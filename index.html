Survival simulation · Team challenge

BUNKER: LAST SHELTER

The world outside has become dangerous. Your team has limited space in the bunker. Reveal your cards, make your case, and convince the group why you deserve to survive.

01 / Set up the game

Choose your players and cards. You can edit the card list below.

Ready. Shuffle the cards to begin.

02 / Players' cards

Pass one device to each player. Each player should reveal their cards only on their turn.

No cards dealt yet. Press “Shuffle & deal cards.”

03 / Debate & vote

Defend your character and explain why you deserve a place in the bunker.

Teacher tip: Decide how many people can enter the bunker before the debate starts. Players should defend their character respectfully.
